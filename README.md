# ds_instacart
Instacart is a grocery delivery platform where customers can place orders and have them delivered, similar to Uber Eats and DoorDash.

In this project, **data wrangling** techniques are applied to the dataset, and we analyze customer purchasing behavior.


### Project Overview:

- **Data Wrangling**: The first step involves cleaning and transforming the dataset, including handling missing values, normalizing variables, and reshaping the data to make it suitable for analysis.
- **Customer Behavior Analysis**: By analyzing the purchasing patterns of customers, we can identify trends in product preferences, frequent purchase times, and order frequency. This analysis helps businesses optimize their marketing strategies and improve customer experience.

### Key Insights:

- Understanding customer preferences allows for targeted promotions and personalized recommendations.
- Identifying the most popular products and peak buying times can help with inventory management and supply chain optimization.
